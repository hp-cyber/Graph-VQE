Readout correction (2026-10-13)

The campaign now reverses CUDA-Q measurement keys (q0 on the left) before
passing probabilities to the Qiskit-based protein decoder (q0 on the right).
readout.json keeps the original counts and now records their bit order plus
the converted protein_probabilities and protein_bit_order.

Standalone measurement readers now consume every SampleResult.items() entry.
Previously their fallback inspected only the most frequent key and one-bit
neighbors, discarding more distant outcomes before renormalizing probabilities.
The two standalone copies and the test source copy have been corrected.

Neither correction changes optimized parameters, measured energies, circuit
connectivity, bond dimensions or shot counts. Existing completed structures
are not rewritten automatically. For old campaign outputs, structures can be
regenerated from readout.json counts without optimization or new measurements.
Old standalone probability files can have missing outcomes and need resampling
from saved parameters before structure regeneration.

Validation: 33 CPU tests; 12 bounded real-protein end-to-end cases spanning
three optimizers, two shot modes and both entanglement orders; actual GPU basis
state bit-order test; three GPU reader checks with widely separated outcomes.
All completed validation cases were invoked again and skipped without changing
their result files. These are bounded checks, not full convergence benchmarks.
